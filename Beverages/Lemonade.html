Lemonade

Ingredients

  • 4 fresh lemons
  • 4 cups chilled water
  • 4 tbsp sugar, or to taste
  • 1/2 tsp black salt
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 4-5 fresh mint leaves
  • 4-5 ice cubes
  • Lemon slices for garnish

Instructions

  1. Wash the lemons thoroughly and cut them in half. Squeeze the juice from the lemons into a bowl, removing any seeds.
  2. Add sugar to a large jug along with a small amount of water. Stir well until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Pour the freshly squeezed lemon juice into the jug and mix thoroughly.
  4. Add the remaining chilled water, black salt, and roasted cumin powder. Stir everything together.
  5. Lightly crush a few fresh mint leaves and add them to the lemonade for a refreshing aroma and flavor.
  6. Add ice cubes and stir once again. Taste and adjust the sugar or lemon juice according to your preference.
  7. Pour the lemonade into chilled glasses and garnish with lemon slices and fresh mint leaves.
  8. Serve immediately and enjoy this refreshing homemade lemonade on a warm afternoon.
