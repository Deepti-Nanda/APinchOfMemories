Lassi

Ingredients

  • 2 cups plain yogurt
  • 1 cup chilled water or milk
  • 3-4 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp fresh cream, optional
  • 1 tbsp chopped pistachios or almonds
  • 4-5 ice cubes
  • 1-2 strands of saffron, optional

Instructions

  1. Add the plain yogurt, sugar, and cardamom powder to a large bowl or blender.
  2. Add chilled water or milk according to your preferred consistency.
  3. Whisk the mixture thoroughly or blend until smooth, creamy, and slightly frothy.
  4. Add the ice cubes and blend briefly until the lassi becomes nicely chilled.
  5. Taste the lassi and adjust the sweetness or consistency according to your preference.
  6. Pour the lassi into chilled glasses. Top with a little fresh cream, chopped pistachios or almonds, and saffron if desired.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy this refreshing drink chilled, especially on a warm afternoon.
