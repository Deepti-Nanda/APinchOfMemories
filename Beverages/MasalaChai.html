Masala Chai

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp black tea leaves
  • 2-3 tsp sugar, or to taste
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
  • 2-3 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 2-3 black peppercorns, lightly crushed

Instructions

  1. Crush the ginger, cardamom, cloves, and black peppercorns lightly using a mortar and pestle.
  2. Add water to a saucepan and bring it to a boil. Add the crushed spices and cinnamon stick.
  3. Reduce the heat and allow the spices to simmer for 2-3 minutes so their flavors infuse into the water.
  4. Add the black tea leaves and simmer for another 1-2 minutes, depending on how strong you prefer your tea.
  5. Pour in the milk and add sugar. Stir well and bring the chai to a gentle boil.
  6. Simmer the chai for 2-3 minutes, allowing the milk, tea, and spices to blend together beautifully.
  7. Strain the chai through a fine tea strainer into cups, removing the tea leaves and whole spices.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your Masala Chai with biscuits, snacks, or your favorite homemade treat.
