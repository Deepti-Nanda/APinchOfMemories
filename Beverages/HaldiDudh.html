Haldi Dudh

Ingredients

  • 2 cups full-fat milk
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1-2 tsp honey or sugar
  • 1/2 tsp grated ginger, optional
  • 4-5 strands of saffron, optional

Instructions

  1. Pour the milk into a saucepan and heat it over medium heat. Do not let it boil too quickly.
  2. Add turmeric powder, black pepper, cinnamon, and grated ginger if using. Stir well to combine.
  3. Allow the milk to simmer gently for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally so that the spices blend well.
  4. Add cardamom powder and saffron if desired. Continue heating for another minute until the milk becomes fragrant.
  5. Turn off the heat and let the milk cool slightly. Remove the cinnamon stick before serving.
  6. Add honey or sugar according to your taste. If using honey, add it after the milk has cooled slightly rather than while it is boiling.
  7. Pour the warm Haldi Dudh into cups and serve immediately. Enjoy it as a soothing, comforting drink, especially on a chilly evening.
