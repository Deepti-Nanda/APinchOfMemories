Kalakand

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh paneer, crumbled
  • 1 can (400 g) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup full-fat milk
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 2 tbsp chopped pistachios
  • 2 tbsp chopped almonds
  • A few saffron strands (optional)

Instructions

  1. Crumble the paneer finely and make sure there are no large lumps. For a softer texture, the paneer should be fresh and moist.
  2. Heat a heavy-bottomed pan and add the crumbled paneer, condensed milk, and milk. Mix everything well.
  3. Cook the mixture over low to medium heat, stirring continuously to prevent it from sticking to the pan.
  4. Continue cooking until the mixture thickens and starts leaving the sides of the pan. It should remain slightly moist rather than becoming completely dry.
  5. Add ghee and cardamom powder. Mix well and cook for another 2-3 minutes.
  6. Grease a flat tray or square container with a little ghee. Transfer the cooked mixture into the tray.
  7. Spread the mixture evenly and gently press it down using a spatula or the back of a spoon.
  8. Sprinkle chopped pistachios, almonds, and saffron strands over the top. Press them gently into the mixture.
  9. Allow the kalakand to cool at room temperature for about 30 minutes. Then refrigerate it for 1-2 hours until it becomes firm enough to cut.
  10. Cut the kalakand into square or diamond-shaped pieces.
  11. Serve chilled or at room temperature and enjoy this creamy, rich Indian sweet.
