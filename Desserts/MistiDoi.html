Misti Doi

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 1/2 cup powdered jaggery
  • 2 tbsp plain yogurt with live cultures
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp water
  • A few saffron strands (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix the whole wheat flour with water to form a soft dough. Set aside.
  2. Pour the milk into a heavy-bottomed pan and bring it to a boil over medium heat.
  3. Reduce the heat and simmer the milk, stirring occasionally, until it reduces to about two-thirds of its original quantity. This helps create a thick and creamy texture.
  4. In a separate pan, add the jaggery and one tablespoon of water. Heat gently until the jaggery melts completely.
  5. Strain the melted jaggery if necessary to remove any impurities. Allow it to cool slightly.
  6. Remove the reduced milk from the heat and let it cool until it is warm but not hot.
  7. Add the melted jaggery and cardamom powder to the warm milk. Mix well until everything is evenly combined.
  8. Allow the mixture to cool further until it is just lukewarm. Add the plain yogurt and gently whisk it into the milk.
  9. Pour the mixture into clean earthen pots or small serving bowls.
  10. Cover the containers and keep them in a warm, undisturbed place for 6-8 hours, or until the yogurt sets firmly.
  11. Once set, refrigerate the Mishti Doi for at least 2 hours to chill and allow the flavor to develop.
  12. Garnish with a few saffron strands or chopped nuts if desired.
  13. Serve chilled and enjoy this traditional Bengali dessert.
