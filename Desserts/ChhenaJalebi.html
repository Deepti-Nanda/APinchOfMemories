Chhena Jalebi

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 tbsp semolina (sooji)
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 3-4 cardamom pods
  • 1 tsp rose water (optional)
  • Oil or ghee for frying

Instructions

  1. Bring the milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Reduce the heat and gradually add lemon juice while stirring gently until the milk curdles.
  2. Strain the curdled milk through a clean muslin cloth. Rinse the chhena with water to remove the sourness from the lemon juice.
  3. Tie the cloth and hang it for about 30 minutes to drain the excess water. The chhena should remain soft and slightly moist.
  4. Transfer the chhena to a plate and knead it gently with your hands for several minutes until it becomes smooth and soft.
  5. Add maida, semolina, baking powder, cardamom powder, and sugar. Mix and knead again to form a soft, smooth dough-like mixture.
  6. Divide the mixture into small portions and roll each portion into a thin rope. Shape the ropes into spiral or coil-like jalebis.
  7. To prepare the sugar syrup, combine sugar and water in a pan. Add cardamom pods and bring the mixture to a boil.
  8. Cook the syrup until it becomes slightly sticky. Add rose water if desired and turn off the heat.
  9. Heat oil or ghee in a deep pan over medium-low heat. Carefully place the chhena jalebis into the hot oil.
  10. Fry the jalebis gently until they become golden brown and evenly cooked on all sides.
  11. Remove the fried jalebis and immediately place them into the warm sugar syrup. Let them soak for 10-15 minutes.
  12. Serve the chhena jalebis warm or at room temperature. They can be enjoyed on their own or with a little extra syrup.
