Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 3-4 tbsp milk, as needed
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 3-4 cardamom pods
  • 1 tsp rose water
  • A few saffron strands (optional)
  • Oil or ghee for deep frying

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine milk powder, all-purpose flour, and baking soda. Mix the dry ingredients well.
  2. Add ghee and gently mix it into the dry ingredients until the mixture becomes slightly crumbly.
  3. Gradually add milk and gently combine everything to form a soft, slightly sticky dough. Do not over-knead the mixture.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 5-10 minutes. Meanwhile, prepare the sugar syrup.
  5. Add sugar and water to a pan. Add cardamom pods and bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  6. Simmer the syrup for 5-7 minutes until it becomes slightly sticky. Add rose water and saffron if desired. Keep the syrup warm.
  7. Divide the dough into small portions and gently roll each portion between your palms to form smooth, crack-free balls.
  8. Heat oil or ghee in a deep pan over low to medium heat. The oil should be moderately hot, not smoking.
  9. Carefully add the dough balls and fry them slowly, turning gently, until they become evenly golden brown.
  10. Remove the fried gulab jamuns and place them on a plate for a minute to drain excess oil.
  11. Transfer the warm gulab jamuns into the warm sugar syrup. Let them soak for at least 1-2 hours so they become soft and absorb the syrup.
  12. Serve warm or chilled. Garnish with chopped pistachios or almonds if desired.
