Roshogulla

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp water
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • 3-4 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 1 tsp rose water, optional

Instructions

  1. Bring the full-fat milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Turn off the heat and allow it to cool slightly.
  2. Mix the lemon juice with a teaspoon of water. Gradually add it to the warm milk while stirring gently until the milk completely curdles and the whey separates.
  3. Line a strainer with a clean muslin cloth and pour the curdled milk through it. Rinse the chhena gently with fresh water to remove the sourness of the lemon juice.
  4. Gather the cloth and squeeze out the excess water. Hang the chhena for about 30-45 minutes until it is moist but not dripping wet.
  5. Transfer the chhena to a clean plate and knead it thoroughly with the palm of your hand for about 8-10 minutes until it becomes smooth and soft.
  6. Divide the chhena into small portions and roll each portion between your palms to make smooth, crack-free balls. Keep them aside.
  7. In a wide saucepan, combine the sugar and water. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil and add the crushed cardamom pods.
  8. Carefully place the chhena balls into the boiling syrup. Cover the pan and cook over medium heat for about 12-15 minutes.
  9. The roshogullas will expand and become soft and spongy. Avoid stirring them directly; gently shake the pan occasionally so they cook evenly.
  10. Turn off the heat and allow the roshogullas to rest in the syrup for a few minutes. Add rose water if desired for extra fragrance.
  11. Chill them before serving or enjoy them slightly warm with plenty of the sweet syrup.
