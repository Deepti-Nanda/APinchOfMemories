Lauki Kofta

Ingredients

  • 2 cups grated bottle gourd (lauki)
  • 1/2 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander, finely chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for frying
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp fresh cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. Grate the bottle gourd and squeeze out the excess water. Keep the squeezed water aside as it can be used while preparing the gravy.
  2. In a bowl, combine the grated bottle gourd, gram flour, red chilli powder, garam masala, cumin seeds, coriander, and salt. Mix well to form a soft mixture.
  3. Shape the mixture into small round koftas. If the mixture feels too loose, add a little more gram flour.
  4. Heat oil in a pan and carefully fry the koftas over medium heat until they turn golden brown and crisp. Remove and keep them aside.
  5. For the gravy, heat a little oil in another pan. Add the chopped onion and sauté until lightly golden.
  6. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute. Then add tomato puree, turmeric powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, red chilli powder, and salt.
  7. Cook the gravy until the oil begins to separate and the tomatoes are well cooked.
  8. Add water and simmer the gravy for a few minutes until it reaches your preferred consistency.
  9. Gently add the fried koftas to the gravy and simmer for 2–3 minutes so they can absorb the flavors.
  10. Add fresh cream if desired and garnish with chopped coriander.
  11. Serve hot with roti, naan, or steamed rice.
