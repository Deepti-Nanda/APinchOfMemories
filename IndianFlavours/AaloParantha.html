Aloo Parantha

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup boiled and mashed potatoes
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil or ghee for cooking

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix the whole wheat flour with water to form a soft dough. Set aside.
  2. In another bowl, combine the mashed potatoes, cumin seeds, garam masala, and salt.
  3. Divide the dough into equal portions and roll each portion into a ball.
  4. Roll out each ball into a small circle, place a spoonful of the potato mixture in the center, and fold the edges over to seal.
  5. Gently roll out the stuffed dough into a flatbread shape.
  6. Heat a skillet over medium heat and cook each parantha until golden brown on both sides, brushing with oil or ghee as needed.
  7. Serve hot with yogurt or pickle.
