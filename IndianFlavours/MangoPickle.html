Mango Pickle

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw green mangoes, cut into small pieces
  • 3 tbsp mustard seeds
  • 2 tbsp fennel seeds
  • 1 tbsp fenugreek seeds
  • 2 tbsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 cup mustard oil

Instructions

  1. Wash the raw mangoes thoroughly and dry them completely. Cut them into small pieces and remove the seeds.
  2. Spread the mango pieces on a clean cloth and allow them to dry for a few hours. Make sure there is no moisture left on the mangoes.
  3. Lightly roast the mustard seeds, fennel seeds, and fenugreek seeds separately. Allow them to cool and coarsely grind them.
  4. In a large bowl, combine the ground spices with red chilli powder, turmeric powder, salt, and asafoetida.
  5. Add the mango pieces to the spice mixture and toss well until every piece is evenly coated.
  6. Heat the mustard oil until it is hot. Turn off the heat and allow the oil to cool completely.
  7. Pour the cooled mustard oil over the mango and spice mixture. Mix everything thoroughly using a clean, dry spoon.
  8. Transfer the pickle into a clean, completely dry glass jar. Make sure the mango pieces are well covered with oil.
  9. Keep the jar in a warm place for several days and stir the pickle occasionally with a clean, dry spoon to help the flavors develop.
  10. Once the mangoes become tender and the spices have blended well, the pickle is ready to enjoy.
  11. Serve with roti, paratha, dal-rice, or any of your favorite Indian meals.
