Okra

Ingredients

  • 250 g fresh okra (bhindi)
  • 1 medium onion, finely sliced
  • 1 medium tomato, finely chopped
  • 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp amchur powder
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander, finely chopped
  • 2-3 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash the okra thoroughly and dry it completely using a clean kitchen towel. Make sure there is no moisture left, as this helps prevent the okra from becoming too slimy.
  2. Trim the ends of the okra and cut them into small pieces. Keep them aside.
  3. Heat 1-2 tablespoons of oil in a wide pan. Add the chopped okra and sauté over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until it becomes slightly crisp. Remove and keep aside.
  4. In the same pan, add a little more oil if needed. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  5. Add the sliced onions and green chillies. Sauté until the onions become soft and lightly golden.
  6. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for about a minute until the raw aroma disappears.
  7. Add the chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Mix everything well.
  8. Cook the masala until the tomatoes become soft and the oil begins to separate from the mixture.
  9. Add the sautéed okra back into the pan and gently mix it with the prepared masala.
  10. Sprinkle garam masala and amchur powder. Cook for another 3-4 minutes on low heat so the okra absorbs the flavors while remaining slightly crisp.
  11. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander and turn off the heat.
  12. Serve hot with roti, paratha, naan, or steamed rice.
