Paneer 65

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper powder
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp thick yogurt
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying
  • 8-10 curry leaves
  • 1-2 green chillies, slit lengthwise
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander, finely chopped

Instructions

  1. Cut the paneer into medium-sized cubes. If the paneer is firm, soak the cubes in warm water for 10 minutes and drain them before using.
  2. In a bowl, combine cornflour, all-purpose flour, ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, black pepper, lemon juice, yogurt, and salt.
  3. Add a little water and mix everything into a thick, smooth coating batter.
  4. Add the paneer cubes to the batter and gently mix until each piece is evenly coated.
  5. Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Carefully add the coated paneer cubes in batches.
  6. Fry the paneer until golden brown and crispy on the outside. Remove and place on a plate lined with kitchen paper to absorb excess oil.
  7. Heat a little oil in another pan. Add curry leaves and slit green chillies. Sauté for a few seconds until fragrant.
  8. Add the sliced onions and sauté until they become slightly soft while still retaining some crunch.
  9. Add the fried paneer cubes and gently toss everything together over medium heat.
  10. Sprinkle a little extra red chilli powder or black pepper if desired. Toss for another 1-2 minutes so the flavors combine well.
  11. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  12. Serve hot as a snack, appetizer, or side dish with your favorite chutney.
